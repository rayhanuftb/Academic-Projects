# Blended, Online and Digital Education Lab

**Course Code:** ICTE 4332  
**Academic Level:** Undergraduate (B.Sc. in Educational Technology and Engineering)  
**Institution:** University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh

---

## 📘 Course Overview

The **Blended, Online and Digital Education Lab** investigates digital pedagogy, asynchronous/synchronous learning architectures, educational content management, and cloud platform deployment for higher education.

---

## 📂 Laboratory Projects

### 1. [Cloud Classroom Platform](./Cloud%20Classroom%20Platform/)
- **Objective:** Full-stack responsive educational learning platform with role-based student and instructor dashboards, course enrollment, lesson progression tracking, assignment submissions, objective quiz auto-scoring, and SQLite database persistence.
- **Technologies:** Node.js, JavaScript (ES6+), SQLite (`node:sqlite`), HTML5, CSS3, REST API.
- **Artifacts:** Full backend (`backend/`), frontend SPA (`frontend/`), relational database (`database/`), automated test suite (`tests/`), and pre-configured demo seed data.
