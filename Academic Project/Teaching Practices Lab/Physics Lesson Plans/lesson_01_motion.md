# Lesson 1: Introduction to Motion

**Subject:** Physics  
**Grade Level:** Class 9  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Topic:** Introduction to Motion

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. Define motion and explain its basic concepts
2. Differentiate between distance and displacement
3. Differentiate between speed and velocity
4. Calculate average speed using the formula: Speed = Distance / Time

---

## Materials and Resources

- Whiteboard and markers
- Projector and slides (if available)
- Ruler and stopwatch
- Student worksheet
- Textbook reference: Chapter on Motion

---

## Teaching Procedures

### 1. Introduction (5 minutes)
- Begin with a question: "When you walk to school, are you moving? How would you describe your motion?"
- Show a short video clip of different types of motion (if available)
- State the lesson objectives

### 2. Direct Instruction (15 minutes)

**Key Concepts:**

**Distance vs Displacement:**
- Distance: Total path traveled (scalar quantity)
- Displacement: Shortest path from start to end (vector quantity)

**Speed vs Velocity:**
- Speed: Distance traveled per unit time (scalar)
- Velocity: Displacement per unit time (vector)

**Formula:**
- Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time
- S = D / T

### 3. Guided Practice (10 minutes)
- Example 1: A student walks 100m to school in 5 minutes. Calculate average speed.
  - Speed = 100m / 5min = 20 m/min
- Example 2: A car travels 150km in 3 hours. What is its speed?
  - Speed = 150km / 3h = 50 km/h

### 4. Independent Practice (10 minutes)
- Worksheet with 5 calculation problems
- Students work individually or in pairs

### 5. Conclusion (5 minutes)
- Recap key concepts
- Assign homework: Calculate the speed of different activities at home

## Differentiation

- **For struggling students:** Provide formula card, worked examples
- **For advanced students:** Challenge with multi-step problems

1. Walking to the nearby shop
2. Riding a bicycle for 2km
3. A bus traveling between two stops
