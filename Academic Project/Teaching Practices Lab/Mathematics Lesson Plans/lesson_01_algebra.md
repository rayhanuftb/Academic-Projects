# Lesson 1: Introduction to Algebraic Expressions

**Subject:** Mathematics  
**Grade Level:** Class 9  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Topic:** Introduction to Algebraic Expressions

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. Define what an algebraic expression is
2. Identify variables, constants, and coefficients in an expression
3. Write algebraic expressions for given word problems
4. Evaluate an expression by substituting values

---

## Materials and Resources

- Whiteboard and markers
- Student worksheet
- Calculator (optional)
- Textbook reference: Algebra chapter

---

## Teaching Procedures

### 1. Introduction (5 minutes)
- Ask: "If I say 'a number plus five', how would you write that using math symbols?"
- Discuss student responses and introduce the concept of variables
- State lesson objectives

### 2. Direct Instruction (15 minutes)

**Key Concepts:**

**Algebraic Expression:** A mathematical phrase containing numbers, variables, and operations.

**Components:**
- **Variable:** A letter representing an unknown value (e.g., x, y)
- **Constant:** A fixed number (e.g., 5, -3)
- **Coefficient:** The number multiplied by a variable (e.g., in 3x, the coefficient is 3)

**Examples:**
| Expression | Variables | Constants | Coefficients |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| 3x + 5 | x | 5 | 3 |
| 2a - 7b + 4 | a, b | 4 | 2, -7 |
| y² + 2y - 1 | y | -1 | 1, 2 |

**Writing Expressions from Words:**
- "Five more than a number" → x + 5
- "Three times a number decreased by 2" → 3x - 2
- "The sum of two numbers" → a + b

### 3. Guided Practice (10 minutes)
- Write expressions for these phrases:
  1. "Seven less than a number" → x - 7
  2. "Twice a number plus nine" → 2x + 9
  3. "The product of a number and four" → 4x

**Evaluation:**
- If x = 3, what is the value of 2x + 5?
- Answer: 2(3) + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11

### 4. Independent Practice (10 minutes)
- Worksheet with 8 problems
- Students translate word phrases to expressions
- Students evaluate expressions for given values

### 5. Conclusion (5 minutes)
- Recap: expression = variable + constant + operation
- Homework: Write 5 expressions from daily life situations

## Differentiation

- **For struggling students:** Provide expression templates
- **For advanced students:** Introduce expressions with two variables

---

## Homework

1. Write algebraic expressions for:
   - The area of a rectangle with length l and width 5
   - The perimeter of a square with side s
   - Six more than twice a number

2. Evaluate 3x² - 2x + 1 when x = 2

---

## Key Formulas

- Expression: combination of variables, constants, and operations
- Substitution: replace the variable with the given value
- Order of operations: PEMDAS/BODMAS applies
