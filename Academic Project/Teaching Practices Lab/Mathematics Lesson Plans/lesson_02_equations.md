# Lesson 2: Solving Linear Equations

**Subject:** Mathematics  
**Grade Level:** Class 9  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Topic:** Solving Linear Equations in One Variable

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1. Define a linear equation in one variable
2. Solve equations using inverse operations
3. Verify solutions by substitution
4. Apply linear equations to solve simple word problems

---

## Materials and Resources

- Whiteboard and markers
- Student worksheet
- Balancing model (optional visual aid)
- Textbook reference

---

## Teaching Procedures

### 1. Introduction (5 minutes)
- Present a balance scale scenario: "If we have x + 3 = 7, how do we find x?"
- Discuss that an equation is like a balanced scale
- State lesson objectives

### 2. Direct Instruction (15 minutes)

**Key Concepts:**

**Linear Equation:** An equation where the variable has a power of 1.

**Inverse Operations:**
| Operation | Inverse |
|-----------|---------|
| Addition | Subtraction |
| Multiplication | Division |

**Solving Steps:**
1. Simplify both sides (if needed)
2. Move variable terms to one side
3. Move constants to the other side
4. Divide by the coefficient of the variable
5. Verify the solution

**Examples:**

**Example 1:** x + 5 = 12
- x + 5 - 5 = 12 - 5
- x = 7
- Check: 7 + 5 = 12 ✓

**Example 2:** 3x = 15
- 3x / 3 = 15 / 3
- x = 5
- Check: 3(5) = 15 ✓

**Example 3:** 2x + 4 = 10
- 2x + 4 - 4 = 10 - 4
- 2x = 6
- 2x / 2 = 6 / 2
- x = 3
- Check: 2(3) + 4 = 10 ✓

### 3. Guided Practice (10 minutes)
- Solve together:
  1. x - 3 = 8 → x = 11
  2. 4x = 20 → x = 5
  3. 2x + 1 = 9 → x = 4

### 4. Independent Practice (10 minutes)
- Worksheet with 8 equations of varying difficulty
- Students solve and verify each answer

### 5. Conclusion (5 minutes)
- Recap: equations are balanced; use inverse operations
- Homework: 5 equations to solve at home

## Assessment

---

## Common Errors to Address

1. Forgetting to apply operation to both sides
2. Sign errors when moving terms
3. Not dividing by the coefficient
4. Not checking the solution

---

## Differentiation

- **For struggling students:** Provide step-by-step template
- **For advanced students:** Introduce equations with fractions

---

## Homework

1. x + 8 = 15

3. 3x - 2 = 13
4. 2x + 6 = 20
5. (x + 4) / 2 = 5
