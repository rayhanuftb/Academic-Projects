# Week 1: Orientation and First Observation

**Date:** Week 1 of Teaching Practice  
**School:** Sample Secondary School  
**Grade Level:** Class 9  
**Subject:** Educational Technology

---

## 1. Description

During the first week of teaching practice, I observed the mentor teacher conducting classes and familiarized myself with the school environment, timetable, and student demographics. The mentor teacher demonstrated effective classroom management techniques and introduced me to the curriculum materials.

**Key Activities:**
- School orientation and introduction to administration
- Observation of 3 class sessions
- Review of curriculum materials and textbooks
- Introduction to classroom management protocols

---

## 2. Feelings

I felt both excited and nervous about starting the teaching practice. Observing the experienced teacher gave me confidence, but I also realized the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical classroom skills. The students seemed curious about the new intern.

---

## 3. Evaluation

**What went well:**
- The mentor teacher provided clear guidance
- Students were welcoming and cooperative
- I gained understanding of the school's teaching approach

**What could be improved:**
- I need to be more proactive in asking questions
- I should have taken more detailed notes during observations

---

## 4. Analysis

The observation period highlighted the importance of preparation and adaptability. The mentor teacher's ability to adjust explanations based on student responses demonstrated skills I need to develop. I noticed that effective teachers use a variety of questioning techniques to engage students.

---

## 5. Conclusion

This week reinforced that teaching requires both content knowledge and pedagogical skill. I need to focus on developing my classroom communication and management skills before my first teaching session.
