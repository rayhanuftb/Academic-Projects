# Week 2: First Teaching Attempt

**Date:** Week 2 of Teaching Practice  
**School:** Sample Secondary School  
**Grade Level:** Class 9  
**Subject:** Introduction to Computer Basics

---

## 1. Description

I conducted my first teaching session on "Introduction to Computer Basics" for a class of 35 students. The lesson covered the definition of a computer, its components, and basic functions. I used a combination of lecture, visual aids, and a short interactive Q&A session.

**Key Activities:**
- Delivered a 30-minute lesson on computer basics
- Used a poster diagram of computer components
- Conducted a Q&A session
- Assigned a short homework activity

---

## 2. Feelings

I was quite nervous at the beginning but gained confidence as the lesson progressed. Some students were very engaged, while others seemed distracted. I felt proud when a student correctly answered my question about input devices.

---

## 3. Evaluation

**What went well:**
- The visual aid helped students understand computer components
- Most students were able to answer basic questions
- I maintained a reasonable pace throughout the lesson

**What could be improved:**
- I spoke too quickly at times
- I didn't check for understanding frequently enough
- Some students were off-task and I didn't address it

---

## 4. Analysis

The experience showed me the importance of pacing and formative assessment. I need to incorporate more "check for understanding" moments. The students who were engaged performed well, suggesting that interactive elements improve learning outcomes.

---

## 5. Conclusion

My first teaching attempt was a learning experience. While the content delivery was adequate, classroom management and pacing need significant improvement. I should incorporate more student-centered activities.

---

## 6. Action Plan

- [ ] Slow down speaking pace
- [ ] Add more check-for-understanding questions
- [ ] Prepare a worksheet for active learning
- [ ] Practice redirecting off-task students
- [ ] Use think-pair-share for the next lesson
